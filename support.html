Common fixes

The five questions that come up most often, and what to do about them.

01My data disappeared after an update or a reset

Your records are stored only in the app's private storage on this iPhone. They do not sync anywhere, and we hold no backup — so if the app was deleted, the device was erased, or "Reset All Data" was tapped, the records are permanently gone.

  • If you restored the app from a device backup, older entries may reappear with their original dates.
  • If you still see the app's data, do not delete the app again — take screenshots first.
  • If the app was deleted and you have no backup, there is no recovery path. We are sorry.

02The dashboard or charts look empty

XM Longview computes everything from the transactions you have entered. It has no market-data feed, so an empty dashboard means there is nothing to compute from yet.

  1. Open Portfolio and confirm at least one holding with a share count and a current price.
  2. Confirm the holding has at least one transaction — shares and cost basis come from transactions, not from the holding header.
  3. Pull down or return to the Dashboard tab to refresh the charts.
  4. Add net-worth snapshots over time to grow the trend line.

03Cost basis, gain/loss, or dividend totals look wrong

All calculations use average cost from the transactions you recorded, with fees added to cost and deducted from proceeds. Small differences usually mean an entry is missing or mistyped.

  1. Open the holding and review every transaction: type, date, shares, price, and fees.
  2. Confirm sells use the correct share count and that dividends record shares plus the per-share amount.
  3. Check for stock splits — record a Split entry when the share count changed without a trade.
  4. Compare the totals against your broker's statement. XM Longview's figures are estimates for personal tracking, not certified figures for filing.

04I cannot attach a photo

Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you attach an image, and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos (or Camera) and set XM Longview to allow access.
  2. Fully close and reopen XM Longview.
  3. Grant only "Selected Photos" if you prefer — attachments work with a limited selection.
  4. Very large photos are automatically downscaled before storage; if an image still fails, try a different one.

05How do I back up or move my data to a new phone

XM Longview has no export function and no cloud backup of its own. Use one of these approaches:

  • Device backup — set up iCloud or a computer backup in iOS Settings. App data is included if the backup is not excluded.
  • Screenshots — capture your holdings, transactions, and journal notes for your own records.
  • Re-enter manually — the fastest route for a handful of holdings and a few transactions.

Frequently asked questions

Answers that do not need a reply from us.

Why is my data in UserDefaults rather than a proper database?

XM Longview is a single-device journal for a modest number of entries. Storing records in the app's own private storage keeps the app dependency-free and means there is no database to migrate, sync, or corrupt. It also keeps everything inside the same protected container as the app itself.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — completely. Once installed, XM Longview never needs a network connection. Every calculation runs on your device.

Can I change the currency shown?

Yes. Open More → Preferences → Display Currency and pick from US Dollar, Chinese Yuan, Euro, British Pound, Japanese Yen, Hong Kong Dollar, Singapore Dollar, Australian Dollar, Canadian Dollar, and Swiss Franc. This changes formatting only; it does not convert values or fetch exchange rates.

Does the app translate my currencies or fetch exchange rates?

No. Amounts are displayed in the currency you select without any conversion. If you hold positions in more than one currency, record them as separate holdings and be careful which currency you read the totals in.

How are splits and dividends handled?

Record a Split transaction when your share count changes without a trade, and a Dividend transaction with the shares held and the per-share amount received. Fees are added to cost basis on buys and deducted from proceeds on sells. Dividends in the last twelve months are totalled on the Analysis tab.

Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

No. XM Longview has no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases, and there is nothing for us to sell because we hold no data about you.

Which iPhones and iOS versions are supported?

iPhone on iOS 16 or later, in portrait orientation. The app is built and tested for iPhone screens.

The numbers differ from my broker's statement. Which is right?

Your broker's official statement is authoritative. XM Longview's figures are calculated only from what you entered, using average cost, so a missing transaction, fee, or split will cause a difference. Reconcile the entries in the holding's transaction list.

Can I recover data after deleting the app?

Not from us — we never held a copy. The only possible route is an iOS device backup that included the app's data. Without one, the data is gone permanently.
