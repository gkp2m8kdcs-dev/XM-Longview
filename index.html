Everything you need to keep a clean record

Version 1.0 ships with the full toolkit for a buy-and-hold investor.

Interactive dashboard

Weighted asset allocation, a six-month net-worth trend line you can drag to inspect, and monthly realised P&L net of contributions — all drawn natively on your device.

Portfolio ledger

Full cost-basis accounting with average cost, realised P&L, dividends and splits. Buys, sells, transfers and income entries with fees, dates and notes.

Research journal

Capture the reasoning behind every decision. Tag notes as Idea, Research, Lesson, Question or Review, link them to a ticker, and attach photos.

Watchlist

Keep an eye on ideas without touching them. Set target prices, track progress with visual bars, and see your highlights on the dashboard.

Five calculators

Compound interest, dollar-cost averaging, return on investment, position sizing and dividend income estimation — each with year-by-year projections.

Analysis & privacy

Sector allocation, concentration risk, top and bottom performers, ten display currencies, light and dark themes — and a one-tap reset that erases everything.

How it works

Four steps, then close the app and get on with your life.

Add your positions

Enter each holding with its symbol, asset class, sector, share count and current price. Group them however you actually think.

Log every transaction

Buys, sells, dividends, splits and transfers. Attach a photo of the confirmation so future-you knows exactly what happened.

Snapshot your net worth

Add a monthly snapshot and watch the trend line build over time. All growth is measured against your own contributions.

Review, don't react

Read the analysis, model scenarios in the calculators, and revisit your journal notes. There is no button to place a trade — by design.

Not investment advice

XM Longview is an educational record-keeping tool. It is not a broker, dealer, exchange, custodian or wallet provider, and it cannot execute or route orders. Historical figures come from data you entered, are not real-time market data, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Verify everything against your broker's statements, and consult a qualified professional before making tax or financial decisions.

Privacy is the product, not a promise

XM Longview was built so that there is nothing to leak in the first place.

What stays on your device

Holdings, transactions, journal notes, photos, watchlist items and net-worth snapshots are written to your app's private storage on this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, mirrored, or backed up to us.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app contains no network code, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see your portfolio because it never reaches us.

Photos you attach

Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you choose to attach an image. Images are compressed and stored inside the app, never transmitted.

If you delete the app

Your data is deleted with it. We cannot restore records we never held. Export or screenshot anything you want to keep before you remove XM Longview.

Common questions

Short answers to what people usually ask first.

Does XM Longview connect to my brokerage?

No. There is no account linking, no Open Banking connection, and no market data feed. Every transaction is entered by you, and every calculation is performed locally.

Where is my data stored?

In the app's private storage on your iPhone. Nothing is synced to iCloud by XM Longview and nothing is sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Is it really free?

There are no subscriptions, no ads and no in-app purchases. There is also nothing for us to sell, because we have no data about you.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone on iOS 16 or later, in portrait orientation. The app is optimised for iPhone screens.

Can I use the numbers for my taxes?

Treat them as personal estimates only. Cost basis and gain/loss figures are derived from your entries and are not certified for filing. Always confirm against official statements and a qualified tax professional.
