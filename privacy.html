01Who we are

XM Longview ("the App", "we", "us") is an independent, single-developer investment journal app for iPhone. We are the publisher of XM Longview and the operator of this website. We are not a broker, dealer, exchange, custodian, wallet provider, or investment adviser.

You can reach us at lueilwitzmarion1262000@gmail.com.

02The short version

Your portfolio does not leave your iPhone. XM Longview has no accounts, no servers, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see your holdings, transactions, notes or photos, and we have no way to obtain them.

We also handle one category of personal data outside the app: the contents of support emails you choose to send us. That is described in Section 6.

04Photos and camera access

XM Longview asks for camera and photo-library permission only when you choose to attach an image to a transaction or a journal note. You may use the app without ever granting these permissions.

  • Photos you attach are compressed and stored inside the app on your device.
  • XM Longview does not upload, analyse, recognise, or transmit your photos.
  • Photos remain in your library; the app does not delete or modify your originals.
  • You can remove any attachment at any time, and removing the app removes the attached copies.

05What we do not collect

To be explicit, XM Longview does not:

  • create or require an account, user ID, or login;
  • collect analytics, telemetry, crash traces, or usage statistics;
  • use advertising, attribution, or cross-app tracking;
  • access the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) or any device fingerprint;
  • integrate with brokerages, banks, exchanges, or market-data providers;
  • embed third-party SDKs, frameworks, trackers, ads, or social widgets;
  • sell, rent, trade, or share personal information — we do not have any to share;
  • require or permit purchases, subscriptions, or in-app transactions.

06Data we receive from you

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include — typically your email address, your device model, your iOS version, and a description of the problem. We use it only to diagnose and answer your question.

Please do not send account numbers, passwords, tax identifiers, or full financial statements. Support does not need them, and we cannot store them securely on our side. Redact screenshots before attaching them.

Our email provider processes this correspondence on our behalf as a basic email host. We do not add recipients, run marketing mail, or build mailing lists from support threads. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the issue and to maintain a record of recurring problems, then delete it.

07How data is stored and protected

App data is written to the private app container on your iPhone and is protected by the device's own security features — a device passcode, and encryption at rest when the device is locked — according to the settings you have configured on your phone.

XM Longview does not encrypt your portfolio with an additional password, and we hold no copy or backup that could be used to recover it. If your device is compromised, your data may be exposed; protect your device with a strong passcode and keep iOS up to date.

08Data retention and deletion

Your app data is retained until you delete it. Specifically:

  • Deleting a holding, transaction, note or snapshot removes it from the app.
  • "Reset All Data" in the More tab permanently erases every holding, transaction, watchlist item, note, photo and snapshot stored on the device.
  • Deleting or uninstalling XM Longview removes the app and its data from the device.
  • Erasing or resetting your device removes the app and its data.
  • Support emails are deleted once the issue is resolved and no longer needed.

These deletions are permanent. Because we never received your portfolio, we cannot restore it. Export, screenshot, or back up anything you want to keep before deleting the app or resetting your device.

09Children

XM Longview is a general-audience record-keeping tool and is not directed at children under 13 or any minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, through the app — the app collects nothing from any device.

10Your rights and choices

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws grant you rights over your personal information. In practice, the design of XM Longview gives you all of them by default, because we hold no personal information about you:

  • Access and portability — your data is on your device, available to you at any time.
  • Correction and erasure — edit or delete any entry inside the app at any time.
  • Deletion — remove everything with "Reset All Data" or by deleting the app.
  • Objection and restriction of processing — stop using the app at any time; we process nothing.
  • Withdraw consent — decline camera or photo access and use the remaining features.
  • Complaint — you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

If you believe we hold information relating to you — for example, in a support email — contact us at the address below and we will respond in line with applicable law.
